## Overview

ReelPlayer is a desktop video player that keeps things simple: open a file and start watching, with a minimal interface that stays out of your way.

## Features

- 🎬 **Smooth playback** — plays your local video files effortlessly
- 🎨 **Minimal interface** — controls appear only when you need them
- 🌙 **Dark & light mode** — follows your system appearance
