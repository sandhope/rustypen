## Overview

SMSFilter is an SMS keyword filter for iOS. Define your own keyword rules and spam messages are quietly moved out of your main inbox.

## Features

- 🔑 **Keyword rules** — filter messages by the keywords you define
- 🔕 **Quiet by design** — spam is filtered silently, no interruptions
- 🔒 **Private** — filtering happens entirely on your device
